Viral rock newcomer Drakonik announces debut album “Hit The Stars”

Internet-born indie rock artist signs to Strixx Records. Album out 21 August 2026.

Strixx Records is proud to announce Hit The Stars, the debut album from indie rock artist Drakonik, released worldwide on 21 August 2026 on all major streaming platforms, CD and limited-edition blue vinyl.

Drakonik first gained attention in late 2025 when he independently released his single “Stop, Drop, and Roll”. The track went viral on social media and reached millions of streams within weeks. He followed it with the emotional “Why Say Why?” and, in 2026, the anthemic “American Fever”.

His sound blends the grit of alternative rock with modern indie influences: heavy guitar riffs, honest, introspective lyrics and choruses made to be shouted back at him. His fans know him by his trademark black beanie and hoodie, and by his motto: “Music for the ones who feel too much.”

The ten-track album explores ambition, escape and the search for something bigger. “This album is about looking up when everything around you is pulling you down,” says Drakonik. “I wrote most of it on a rooftop at 3 a.m.”

Drakonik will celebrate the release with a live-streamed rooftop concert on release night, followed by a 20-date tour.

Exclusive interview

in his own words

Drakonik rarely talks to the press. We met him backstage before his sold-out show to talk about going viral, keeping his name secret and the new album.

“I'd rather be too loud than not heard at all.”

Where does the name Drakonik come from?

DRAKONIKIt comes from “draconian”, which means really strict rules. I grew up feeling like there were rules for everything: how to act, what to feel. The name reminds me to break them.

Why keep your real name a secret?

DRAKONIKI want people to focus on the music, not on me. Drakonik is the part of me that's brave enough to get on stage. The other guy just likes video games and cereal.

“Stop, Drop, and Roll” went viral almost overnight. How did that feel?

DRAKONIKHonestly? Terrifying. I uploaded it at 2 a.m. and went to sleep. When I woke up my phone wouldn't stop buzzing. A week later strangers were sending me videos of themselves screaming the chorus in their cars.

Your motto is “music for the ones who feel too much.” What does that mean?

DRAKONIKSome people get told they're too sensitive, too emotional, too much. I was one of them. My songs say: that's not a weakness. Feel it, scream it, turn it into something.

What is “Hit The Stars” about?

DRAKONIKAmbition and escape. It's about sitting on a rooftop, looking at a city that doesn't notice you, and deciding to aim higher anyway.

What's next?

DRAKONIKA rooftop concert on release night, then the tour. I just want to be in the same room as the people who've been listening all this time.
